## 0.3.0, 2026-09-15

The rest of the intake is real. Music: an `[audio]` section (`enabled`; `files` in play order, relative to the project folder or absolute, each checked by the `show` stage; `loop`; `crossfade_s`; `fade_s`; `volume`) reaches `show.json` with the paths made absolute, and the 0.1 key `[show] audio = true` without that section is an error naming it; `bin/prep` transcodes each file to `build/audio/NN-<stem>.m4a` and records it in the prep manifest; `bin/render` still renders and verifies a silent file, now named with `-silent` before `.mp4`, then joins the tracks with crossfades into one playlist, cuts a soundtrack of the video's length with the fades and the volume (the playlist looped when asked), muxes it on under the plain name and probes the result; `render --concat` joins the silent copies and lays one soundtrack over the whole file, so the music runs across the loop seams; the live player plays the same tracks, at once where the browser allows it and otherwise from the first key press or click, and M mutes. The VLC launchers no longer pass `--no-audio`: a silent file plays nothing and a file with music plays it, so the runbook says to set the venue volume. The cap falls out of the loop length: `[selection] cap_per_year = 0` (or missing) derives it from `[show] loop_minutes_target` at 2.2 seconds per item, the first run's 469 moments in 15 minutes at medium tiles and 100 px/s, scaled by the tile size and the scroll speed, over the year-shares in scope; select prints the arithmetic on one line, `show.json` carries the plan under `selection`, and `[selection.cap_overrides]` gives single years their own cap. `[show] scope_start` and `scope_end` set the scope and pro-rate the first and last years from their months, so a birth year is no longer required; a config with no scope at all stops with the three keys named. The taste answers act: `[taste] order` (`chapters`; `chronological` for one chapter; `shuffled` on `[taste] seed`, named in the build report and `sequence.txt`), `motion_density` (`calm`, `normal`, `busy`: 2, 4 or 6 tiles moving at once and 1, 2 or 3 in a base row), `mixed_tiles = false` (no featured picks, no anchors, every row a base row with the videos in the grid), `include_videos`, `include_gifs` and `live_photos = "still"` (the types left out are cut with the new reason `excluded-type`; a Live Photo whose clip is cut competes as a plain still and is handed off as one, with `HANDOFF.md` naming the count), and `slow_motion` (`slow` or `realtime`, with per-file exceptions in `<project>/prep-options.json`, read before the repository's copy). `[selection.weights]` and `[selection.featured]` expose the v4 weights and the featured thresholds with the first run's values as defaults. `live_photos_full` leaves the example config; a 0.2 `config.toml` that still has it is read as `live_photos` (`true` clip, `false` still) with a warning.

Also new: sidecars for every export. Folder sources (a folder tree, an Apple Photos export, an iCloud download, an unzipped Takeout) index the Takeout JSON and the `.xmp` sidecars beside their files into `index/takeout-sidecars.csv`, with a `source` column, and leave them and `.aae` files out of the copy, so an Apple export made with "Export IPTC as XMP" brings its dates, places and the names of people; `source_path` for a folder source has the same `<label>!<path>` shape as a zip's `<zip>!<member>`, and the index looks both up alike. `people_tags` in `items.csv` carries the sidecar's names, and `identify --tags-only` turns them into `people` and `family_present` against `[family] names` without the detection models (a tag matches a name when equal case-insensitively, or when a one-word name equals the tag's first word), so `[family] gate = "family"` runs on any tagged export; the sheets print the matched names. `[priors] folder_year_rule` dates a file no better rung could to a four-digit year in its folder's name, precision year, witness named. `[machines] player = "tv-usb"` makes the render add the H.264 profile and level a TV stick decodes and warn about any final file of 4 GiB or over; `"browser"` has kiosk launchers of its own, `start-slideshow-browser.command` and `.bat`. Still recorded and not acted on: captions, the theme, `[show] playback`, `[show] hard_stop`, `[taste] videos_silent`, `[family] seed_photos`, `[models] face_embedder` and `[tools] vlc`. Three new stops keep a stale or impossible state from reaching the player: `bin/build` refuses to build on a prep output that failed its check, or on clips prepared under the other `slow_motion` mode while a slow-motion capture is in the set (a warning when there is none); `select` refuses `[family] gate = "people"` when `people.csv` carries no person counts, as after `identify --tags-only`, and names the detection run and the other two gates; and `add-item` refuses to form a Live Photo pair under `live_photos = "still"`. A prepared track's name keeps letters, digits, dot, dash and underscore and turns anything else into an underscore, so a music title with a `#` or an apostrophe still loads in the live player. The references correct the loop estimate to about 2 seconds per item at medium tiles, not 3, and the intake questions in `SKILL.md` and `references/00-intake.md` still say the same thing, question for question.

## 0.2.0, 2026-09-15

The settings a user gives at setup now reach the build. The intake recorded `[output]`, `[taste]` and `[show]` answers in `config.toml` that nothing read; they are now derived in Python into `handoff/show.json` (written by the new `python curate/run.py show` stage and by handoff; the contract, with every derivation rule, is in `references/02-index-contract.md`), and every build-side command reads its numbers from there: the viewport and frame rate from `[output] resolution` and `fps`; row heights from `[taste] tile_size` as a share of the screen height (`small`, `medium`, `large`; `medium` is the first run's 520 and 820 px at 1440 rows), with `base_row_height` and `feature_row_height` as pixel overrides; the maximum tile height, the gutter and the scroll speed scaled to the screen; `[taste] background` painted on the page, the stage and every rendered frame; the x264 preset from `[output] quality` (`final` or `draft`) and the number of concatenated copies from `concat_copies`. Without `show.json` the build and the render run on the first run's defaults and say so in one warning; `bin/build` also warns when the tiles were prepared for a different maximum height. The player no longer hard-codes 2560x1440 anywhere, and the review type scales with the height. Step 0 (`curate/setup.py`) detects the machine's timezone and the logical resolution of its display, and with `--project <folder> --apply` writes them into `config.toml` where it is blank, together with `[machines] build_os`; without `--apply` it prints the lines to paste. `[machines] display_resolution` is gone; `[output] resolution` replaces it. `[machines] player` now accepts `vlc`, `tv-usb` or `browser` and is checked; the 0.1 spelling `tv` is read as `tv-usb` with a warning.

Also new: `[show] off_limits` (filenames, media ids, or a file or folder path) is cut by select with reason `off-limits` before any other test and refused by `add-item`, in replacement rounds too; `[show] must_include` seats like `[pins] files`; `[family] gate = "none"` skips the people check, and the stage says which gate ran; `.webp` stills and `.avi`, `.mkv`, `.mts`, `.m2ts`, `.3gp`, `.webm`, `.wmv`, `.mpg` and `.mpeg` videos are accepted, from one list of extensions in `common.py` that every stage and the build share (a webp goes through `curate/heic.py` everywhere, since `sips` is not trusted with it); `sheets --pdf` bundles the contact sheets into `index/sheets/contact-sheets.pdf`, one page per sheet, for an owner who is not at the machine; `render --labels` draws the review labels into every frame and names the file `-labels`; `render --concat` (`--x3` still works) writes `slideshow-x<N>.mp4`, and the launchers look for `slideshow-x*.mp4` first, then `slideshow.mp4`; `[tools] chrome` names the browser the render starts, before Playwright's bundle and installed Chrome. Music, the theme, mixed tiles, the Live Photo and silent-video answers and captions are recorded in the config and marked as not read by this version. The skill and the references describe all of this and nothing more; the intake questions in `SKILL.md` and `references/00-intake.md` now say the same thing, question for question.

Fixed: `requirements.txt` pinned `numpy==2.5.3`, which needs Python 3.12 while the README promises 3.11, so setup failed on 3.11 with "No matching distribution". It is now `numpy==2.4.6`, which supports 3.11 and 3.12 and later; `tzlocal` is added for the timezone detection; and a failed pip install now names the Python that ran it, so a version mismatch is visible.

Exercised on this version by the smoke test on Linux, on a synthetic project of fourteen generated JPEGs, a .webp, a .png, two .mp4 clips, a .mkv and a gif: every stage but identify (ingest, index, validate, select with gate none, sheets and `sheets --pdf`, handoff and `show`), an off-limits cut, a must-include pin, the .webp, the .png, the .mkv and the owner-dated gif through prep, `bin/build` at 1280x720 and 30 fps, a three-second labelled render and the full-loop render with `render --concat` through a Chromium named in `[tools] chrome`, the add-item refusal, the no-show.json defaults and `setup.py --apply`. Not exercised: the identify stage (no detection models installed), a Takeout zip source, the Windows `.cmd`/`.bat` wrappers and launchers, the macOS `sips` and VLC paths and the `.command` launchers on a real Mac, and timezone and display detection on a real desktop (both report "not detected" in a container).

## 0.0.3, 2026-09-08

The stages are real. `curate/run.py <stage>` runs `ingest`, `index`, `validate`, `identify`, `select`, `sheets` and `handoff` under `curate/stages/`, each wired to `common.py`, resumable, dry-runnable, printing its counts and refusing to write when its accounting does not balance. New in the port: the validate stage from `references/03` (stem-join witness, era plausibility, prefix-versus-metadata witness, Live Photo pair gate, Takeout title collisions, provisional owner dates); Takeout sidecars indexed inside the zips and keyed by member path; `media_id` (SHA-256) as the identity from ingest to the build ledger; HEIC handled in Python (`curate/heic.py`, pillow-heif) with `sips` kept as the macOS fast path; `curate/setup.py` (venv, pinned `requirements.txt`, YuNet and YOLOv8n fetched and exported at setup with a license manifest, toolchain report, optional Windows ffmpeg fetch).

Build side: every command resolves the project folder (`--project`, `SLIDESHOW_PROJECT`, or the nearest `config.toml`) and never writes into the repository; `add-item` computes `media_id` and writes contract-format `changes.log` lines; `apply-replacements` writes `media.csv` and `changes.log` back into the round folder on every run; Windows `.cmd` wrappers with a `SetThreadExecutionState` keep-awake; Playwright's bundled Chromium tried before installed Chrome.

Tested on Windows with synthetic media only: all seven stages end to end, then `prep` and `build` on the handoff. Untested: ffmpeg paths, detection on real photos, macOS. The legacy scripts stay in `curate/legacy/` until the smoke test passes.
